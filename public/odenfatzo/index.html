'Someone Like You' · early November

A rocket, a village and two dreamers in flight suits.

An offbeat mockumentary in the style of a cult French TV documentary. The duo play two neighbours who sell leeks, wear cosmonaut suits as house clothes and build a rocket from salvage — their space world, told as a story.

YouTube features referenced, as documented

  • Collaborations — up to 10 creators per video; long-form, Shorts and archived streams; the posting channel keeps revenue. Help
  • Shorts related video — "Select a Related Video from your channel"; needs advanced features. Help
  • Live Redirect — routes: live stream → Premiere; live stream → another channel's live stream; Premiere → another Premiere; Premiere → another channel's live stream. Sender: 1,000+ subscribers, no strikes. Receiver allows redirects in Studio (Settings › Community: subscribed channels by default, all channels, or up to 100 named). Set up in Studio › Content › Live › Edit › Customization › Redirect; fires when the source ends. Same-channel Premiere → live isn't listed; YouTube suggests a pinned chat message. Help
  • Premium afterparties — live streams with artists, hosted video and chat, for YouTube Premium and Music Premium members; archives may stay available to members. The page doesn't describe how artists are selected. Help
  • Premieres are already used on Defected for Lady Love, Fly Away, Only You (Live Session), Pacha and Wembley. Chapters were not found on the long sets checked; captions not checked.
