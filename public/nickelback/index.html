What to doIdea · a Nickelback YouTube Station

Turn catalogue viewing
into album viewing.

What is a YouTube Station? An always-on stream of the band’s videos, programmed like a radio station. Fans tune in for the hits; the new album plays in between.

Why

The catalogue is where the viewing is: How You Remind Me alone has 612.8M views.

The mix

Alternate a hit with a new song, as above. Every album track, plus the three singles.

Keep it fresh

Add each new video as it lands: Chasin Famous, the acoustic Make Me Love You, If I Don’t Go.

Tour mode

From January, lean into live: Red Rocks, Live From Nashville and the songs on the setlist.

Method and gaps

Catalogue: every public upload from the YouTube Data API through our Watcher integration (688 rows, uncapped), read 2026-11-20T14:10Z. The campaign window follows the plan: 14 Jul (first single) to 20 Nov.

Watcher: 180 daily channel snapshots (21 May–20 Nov 2026) and per-video daily series. Watcher's per-video series stopped on 2 Nov (Rattle The Cage video), 29 Oct (Rattle visualiser, Best Of) and 13 Nov (Leave Me Behind visualiser); 30-day gains are only quoted for series that run to 20 Nov.

Plan: Plan created 29 Jun 2026, last edited 17 Sep 2026; no earlier version stored. The Coach shows Make Me Love You with music video, visualiser and Shorts, and no lyric or live/acoustic version.

Lifetime views are totals at 20 Nov, not views earned inside the campaign. Not used: retention, click-through, traffic sources or paid/organic splits. These need Studio.
