The window we look at

When a video lands, attention spikes — and the first 7–14 days are when it's still there to build on. That's the window we'd plan into every single.

10 · Across the album · dates to come

Not every song gets the same plan.

Hero singles

The full run

Two or three songs

  • Teasers → Premiere with pre-party → official video
  • Lyric video in the 7–14 day window
  • Live version within the month
  • Shorts throughout
Other singles

One strong video

Lyric video as the hero, like Roses

  • Lyric video or visualiser
  • Two or three Shorts
  • A live clip if one exists
Album week

A home for every track

As Screen Violence did

  • Every track up on release day
  • Album playlist live
  • A celebration stream with the band

Put the effort behind two or three songs. The rest still get a proper video and a way in.
