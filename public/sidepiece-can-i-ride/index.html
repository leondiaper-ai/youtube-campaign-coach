05 · The creator-to-video pathway

Sound pages matter. Related Shorts barely register.

52,810OMV views · sound pages
288OMV views · related Shorts
131,336OMV views · search
140,829OMV views · browse

What is already working

Sound pages account for 8.4% of OMV views. That is a measurable bridge from music discovery to the video. Search is another substantial route at 20.9%.

What needs testing

Related Shorts drove only 288 views, less than 0.1% of the OMV total. Audit whether SIDEPIECE's own Shorts have related-video links and test clear creative hand-offs to the OMV.

Don't promise a massive Shorts-to-OMV conversion uplift. Fix the links, test the pathway, then measure whether referrals actually increase.

YouTube Studio traffic-source report, 14 Oct–10 Nov. Related Shorts: 288 / 628,731 = 0.046%. Sound pages: 52,810 / 628,731 = 8.4%.

09 · The multi-format lesson

One record. Several ways to discover the artist.

What Defected demonstrates

In the prior Edge Of Desire audit, the release was connected across official audio, lyric, live performance, remixes and Shorts. The value is in linking complementary formats, not hitting a video quota.

How that applies here

Discover: creator-led Shorts and existing dance/social behaviour.

Recognise: SIDEPIECE joining the trend or performing the song live.

Continue: the OMV and existing relevant supporting assets.

Only build connections between real assets. A playlist or related-video link cannot replace compelling creative.

Strategic comparison with the existing Defected deep dive. No measured uplift is attributed to the format mix.

10 · What Insomniac can do now

Use the momentum. Prove what converts.

01 · Creator audit

Find the winning 20

Capture creators, views, engagement, geography and the exact creative hook. Prioritise 3–5 relevant creators for a second wave.

02 · Artist connection

Make SIDEPIECE visible

Test two artist-led creator formats. Check existing live footage for one strong track-specific performance edit and related Shorts.

03 · Measurement

Separate reach from conversion

Use the Studio baseline: 628.7k OMV views, 29.9% ads, 52,810 sound-page views and 288 Related Shorts views. Test links and track referral lift, search and returning viewers.

The creator trend is already sending 52.8k OMV views through sound pages. Grow that route, test the underused Related Shorts pathway and measure repeat listening separately.
