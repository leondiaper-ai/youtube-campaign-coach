07 / INSOMNIAC ACTION PLAN

What to do while the trend is moving.

NOW · CREATOR AUDIT

Find the winning 20

Record creator, country, views, engagement and concept for 20 leading relevant Shorts. Select 3–5 creators whose formats already work. Test two artist-led responses.

NEXT 7 DAYS · CONTENT

Make the artist visible

Locate a strong “Can I Ride” live moment. If available, publish one performance edit and 2–3 Shorts. Audit official Shorts for sound and related-video links.

IMMEDIATELY · MEASUREMENT

Prove the conversion

Request organic daily OMV views, paid split, Shorts referrals and subscriber contribution before/after the October acceleration. Scale only what works.

The goal is not more content for its own sake. It is to turn the 98k-sound-use moment into measurable interest in SIDEPIECE.
