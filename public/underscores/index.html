How this was measured

Evidence, method and limitations behind the presentation. Channel figures from the public YouTube Data API v3 read on 16 October 2026 unless dated otherwise. No YouTube Studio data was available.

Inventory

All 102 public uploads on @underscores, paged to exhaustion from the uploads playlist — not a sample. Channel opened July 2013. Lifetime channel views 36,814,369; public subscriber count 145,000 (rounded by YouTube).

The channel's most-viewed upload is a 2014 production video (Virtual Riot makes a dubstep drop in 10 minutes, 2.14M). It predates the current artist project and is left out of the slides.

Channel setup — what is already in place

  • Official Artist Channel — confirmed by YouTube's "Official Artist Channel" badge on the channel page, not inferred from the Releases tab.
  • 11 public playlists, including album playlists for U, Wallsocket and fishmonger, a Music Videos playlist and the Wsk Life series.
  • Home tab (signed-out view): trailer, then shelves for ‘U’ The 3rd Album, Music Videos, Wsk Life and Posts. The API reports seven channel sections.
  • Trailer: underscores – Unlimited Love (♫), an audio upload (254,465 views). The returning-subscriber featured video is not visible signed-out and should be checked in Studio.
  • Tabs: Home, Videos, Shorts, Live, Releases, Playlists, Posts.

YouTube's help centre describes the trailer as shown to people who aren't subscribed, and the featured video as shown to returning subscribers; each stops showing once a viewer has watched it. A more-viewed video is not automatically a better trailer, so the change is framed as a test.

Comment research — method

  • 160 comments (40 per single) from the four singles, ordered by YouTube's relevance ranking, coded by reading in the 16 Oct audit: album/era 25, video/visuals 21, production/sound 9, live/touring 6.
  • Relevance ordering favours liked and replied-to comments — not a random sample. No sentiment score.
  • A re-read on 16 Oct returned 40 each for The Peace, Music and Tell Me; Do It returned none on that call. Quoted comments were re-checked against that re-read, and the creative-director comment is on The Peace (an earlier draft placed it on Do It).
  • Quotes illustrate what some fans notice. They do not establish what drives performance.

Limitations

  • No Studio metrics: no impressions, click-through, retention, average view duration, traffic sources, unique or returning viewers, territory split or subscribers by video.
  • Measurement in the priorities uses metrics Studio reports to the channel owner (views, average view duration, percentage viewed, traffic sources, geography, subscribers). Which of these can be isolated for a given test is to be confirmed before targets are set. A trailer before/after read is directional.
  • Tour status is dated internal reporting (14 Oct 2026). The pop-up, tour marketing and the 2027 album timing, budget and rollout are all TBC.
  • Any filming recommendation is subject to artist agreement, crew, cost and clearance.
