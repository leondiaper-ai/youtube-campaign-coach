Comment research — method

160 comments retrieved 16 October 2026 via the public commentThreads endpoint — 40 each from The Peace, Do It, Music and Tell Me (U Want It), ordered by YouTube's relevance ranking.

  • Relevance ordering is not a random sample. It favours comments with likes and replies, so it over-represents popular opinion.
  • No sentiment score was computed. Themes were coded by reading.
  • Coding counts: album or era 25, video or visuals 21, production or sound design 9, live or touring 6.
  • The low live count is not evidence of weak tour interest — the UK shows had not happened and relevance ordering buries recent comments.

Artist-surface evidence

Claims about authorship and era grammar come from the public video descriptions and titles, read with desc=full on 16 Oct 2026:

  • Creative-director credit — the description of underscores — The Peace lists "creative director / underscores" alongside director Ayodeji, production company bigkid, dp Shane Bagwell. It also opens with a flashing-lights warning.
  • Artwork — "Artwork by underscores" appears on two current-era uploads; photography credited to Lulu Syracuse, live visuals to beanlette.
  • Writing locations — the Unlimited Love description states the record was written and recorded in Chicago, Phoenix, Austin, Houston, Seattle, "on the I–10, and on the I–20".
  • Chaptered streams — both production streams carry five song timestamps in the description, linking to underscores.plus/u.
  • Era grammar — observed across all 102 public titles. 2021 uses bare lower-case song names; 2023 uses underscores 'Song' Lyric Video; 2026 uses an em dash for videos and ‎ (♫) for audio. One 2024 upload carries a raw working filename and no description.
  • Prior platform relationship — two Shorts titled underscores 'YouTube Music' billboard were published in September 2023.

These are descriptive observations about how the channel is authored. They are not performance claims.

Other surfaces

An auto-generated underscores — Topic channel carries 13,797,333 lifetime views on 549 subscribers. Topic channels are generated from distributed audio and behave differently from an artist channel; the subscriber count is not a meaningful signal and no configuration fault is implied. These views are counted separately and never added to the 36.8M artist-channel figure.

Collaborators appearing in the catalogue: Jane Remover, gabby start, henhouse!, 8485, Knapsack, Yves, Frost Children. Uploads on collaborators' own channels are a separate surface, not counted here.

Third-party and fan uploads were not systematically mapped. No third-party view is owned, controlled or monetised by the artist.

Reporting limitations

  • No YouTube Studio metrics. No impressions, click-through, retention, average view duration, traffic sources, unique viewers, returning viewers, territory split or subscriber gain by video. Every such question is listed as a request, not estimated.
  • Publication order is not a funnel. Where Shorts preceded a video, that is a sequence. Movement between assets cannot be measured without traffic-source data.
  • Length and like rate do not indicate retention. The production stream's view count shows it attracted an audience, not how long that audience stayed.
  • Shorts and long-form view counts are not directly comparable. YouTube changed Shorts view counting in March 2025, and the formats are surfaced differently.
  • Channel lifetime total exceeds the sum of public uploads — 36,814,369 against 23,330,281. The difference is not explained by public data and is not attributed to any specific cause here. No figure in the presentation is expressed as a share of lifetime views.
  • Production, rights and budget are not assumed. Any filming recommendation is subject to crew, cost, clearance and artist agreement.
  • Snapshot. Figures move. Newest upload at time of reading: 28 August 2026.
