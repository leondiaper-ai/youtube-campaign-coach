Related Video requires advanced features and eligible content on the artist’s own channel. Premieres can support a shared launch if the band can participate. Post-release guidance supports planning across formats, not a fixed universal upload quota.

Confirm release dates and artwork, footage and music rights, availability, production/edit budget, Studio access and Topic delivery. VMG will pitch release support, Top Fans, London OOH, Music Night / release party and Stations, backed by active artist participation and the proposed channel programme. Sky TV support is a separate pitch route when a documentary, full show or other marquee asset is ready for a bigger launch. No support is confirmed; partner approval, lead times, inventory and rights still apply. The six-week plan is our proposed deliverable programme, not a verified universal eligibility rule.
