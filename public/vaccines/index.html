The evidence
behind the story.

Source, scope and dates

Existing Campaign Coach / Watcher YouTube Data API catalogue, captured 9 Nov 2026 at 15:25 UTC. @thevaccinesuk · UCWLY9PpOMnI3Q66zwZdyzyA. The uploads playlist was exhausted: 241 unique entries, uncapped. Views are lifetime at capture, not campaign-period or age-matched results.

Observed last-album window: 20 Sep 2023 teaser to 12 Apr 2024 last retrieved clip. The official release was 12 Jan 2024. These are analytical boundaries. Eight identified song assets cover five of ten album tracks in this list; Topic/audio, partner and fan coverage is not comprehensive.

The last album: format, cadence and follow-up

One music video: 299,342. Four primary visualisers: 723,014. Three lyric visualisers: 68,474. Total 1,090,830; primary visualisers hold 66.3%. Titles and description first lines were reviewed: Lunar Eclipse and Discount De Kooning are visualisers; Heartbreak Kid is the music video.

Through album day: 8 full-song videos and 13 short clips. The three lyric versions all landed on 20 Dec 2023: 83, 65 and 33 days after their corresponding primary videos. No identified same-song long-form follow-up at +7–14 days across the five primary song videos. After album day, 13 Jan–12 Apr: 7 clips and 0 new album long-form uploads in the retrieved list.

Proposed next cadence: four full-video slots over six weeks (week 1, week 2, week 4, week 6), plus two distinct Shorts and one Community post per week. A 7–14 day follow-up is an operating test, not a measured optimum. Different songs, ages, spend and contexts prevent causal conclusions about formats or timing.

Catalogue: the 32.7m denominator

Six selected debut-era videos: If You Wanna 12,335,543; Post Break-Up Sex 9,324,875; Norgaard 3,940,750; Wetsuit 3,625,838; Wreckin’ Bar 2,217,259; All In White [Censored] 1,227,183. Total 32,671,448. It is selected-video lifetime viewing, not total album consumption, current traffic share, unique fans or a conversion forecast.

The debut was released in March 2011. The 2026 anniversary is already being used in touring and channel uploads. The promoter’s 15th-anniversary listing includes November shows. The proposed warm-up continues this existing story; it does not present the March anniversary as a future event.

Live content: precedent and caution

The three 2018 sessions Your Love Is My Favourite Band, Put It On A T-Shirt and Nightclub total 1,193,533 lifetime views. Seven Reading 2018 archive songs uploaded in 2026 total 40,243. Different ages and contexts make this an unsuitable efficiency comparison; it supports a limited new-song performance test rather than a blanket live-series commitment.

Data limitations and growth excluded

Retrieved rows sum to 74,525,862 versus a 91,649,008 channel counter: a 17,123,146 gap. Channel counter 122 public videos versus 241 retrieved OAC/linked entries. Scopes differ; this is not a census of all Vaccines viewing.

The same 90,738,043 channel total repeats from 15 Oct through 8 Nov, then refreshes to 91,649,008 on 9 Nov. The displayed +910,965 weekly delta is excluded as a pace measure. No 2023–24 subscriber history supports historical campaign subscriber-growth claims.

Short clips use a legacy duration proxy ≤62 seconds; all 20 in the last-album window are under 60 seconds. Shorts-feed placement, clicks, conversion, paid/organic splits, retention, returning viewers and asset-level subscriber attribution are not established. Shorts counters span the 2025 counting change.

Platform mechanics and open decisions

Related Video requires advanced features and eligible content on the artist’s own channel. Premieres can support a shared launch if the band can participate. Post-release guidance supports planning across formats, not a fixed universal upload quota.

Confirm release dates and artwork, footage and music rights, availability, production/edit budget, Studio access and Topic delivery. Weekly Release Support, Top Fans, OOH, Music Night and Station remain partner conversations; screenshot scores and requirements are not verified eligibility.
