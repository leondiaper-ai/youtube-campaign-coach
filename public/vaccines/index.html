What the data
can actually tell us.

Source, scope & timing

Built in the existing Campaign Coach / Watcher repository, using its YouTube Data API catalogue route. Captured 9 November 2026, 15:25 UTC. Channel: @thevaccinesuk, UCWLY9PpOMnI3Q66zwZdyzyA. Playlist exhausted: 241 returned upload entries; no cap reached.

The 2023–24 observed window runs from the 20 September teaser to the last retrieved clip on 12 April. The eight identified song videos represent five album tracks. Twenty short clips in the same window include tour promotion. The boundary is an analytical choice.

All asset views are lifetime at capture. They are not campaign-period earnings and are not age matched. Existing public titles and description first lines establish the three previously unlabelled formats.

Coverage & the channel-total gap

The retrieved rows sum to 74,525,862 views. The channel lifetime counter is 91,649,008; a gap of 17,123,146. OAC/linked inventory and the public-video counter have different scopes. The 241 rows are not a complete census of all Vaccines viewing, deleted/private videos, Topic, partner and fan uploads.

Shares use explicit retrieved-upload or eight-asset denominators. No album-track absence is claimed across all YouTube. Public access also does not establish ownership or reuse rights.

Why weekly growth is excluded

Watcher has 26 daily readings from 15 October to 9 November. The same 90,738,043 total repeats through 8 November, then changes to 91,649,008. This could contain accumulated movement after a cache refresh. The displayed +910,965 seven-day change is not reliable evidence of seven days’ consumption.

No 2023–24 subscriber history was available. This review cannot substantiate the brief’s historical campaign subscriber-growth claim, estimate past conversion or attribute current channel movement to a song.

Comparisons & Shorts

Different songs, release dates, paid support and upload contexts can explain differences in lifetime views. Format mix, selected catalogue examples and timing gaps are descriptive. There is no controlled evidence that a visualiser beats a music video, an earlier lyric upload improves views, or a particular cadence causes growth.

The legacy short-clip proxy is duration ≤62 seconds; all twenty in the campaign window are under 60 seconds. It does not prove Shorts-feed distribution. Shorts view definitions changed in 2025; raw 2024 and 2026 counts should not be compared as like-for-like performance. CTR, retention, unique/returning viewers and paid/organic splits need Studio.

What still needs confirming

Next album and single dates, artwork, production budget, the band’s availability, existing footage and rights, actual platform access, Top Fans mechanics, partner deliverables, UK country demand, and the label’s existing Topic/audio delivery.
