How we make it useful

Start with the programme.
Ask for the platform.

01 / Curate now

A playlist first

Build a proposed order from videos already on YouTube, add the new release when delivered, and give the playlist a channel-home shelf. VMG curates; management approves. This does not require rebroadcasting the files.

02 / Confirm access

The Station conversation

Ask YouTube whether the channel can access Stations and what delivery, duration, operation and reporting requirements apply. Check the rights position with management, label and relevant video partners.

03 / Refresh deliberately

New music in the run

Update the new-release slots as the campaign evolves. Refresh the entry point and the live selection; don’t assume a 33-minute sample can simply become a 24/7 product without programming and operational work.

Who owns it?

VMG: proposed sequence and partner brief. Management / label: artist approval and rights map. Video partners: permissions and deliverable checks. YouTube: eligibility, setup and supported reporting.

What would success mean?

Use the reporting YouTube actually makes available to assess repeat viewing and the programme’s role in new-release discovery. For the playlist version, inspect playlist traffic and watch time in Studio. No uplift or audience size is assumed.
