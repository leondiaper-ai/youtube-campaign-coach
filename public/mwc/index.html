Man/Woman/Chainsaw × YouTube04 / What is working
What needs attentionPrioritised · public channel, 20 Nov

Five fixes
before the show goes out.

  1. 01

    The full show is hidden.

    Uploaded unlisted on 27 Oct. 4 views, no chapters. The show was 7 Oct, so it gets older every week.

  2. 02

    Three long-form uploads since album day.

    In 105 days: Still Angry live, a Goddamn audio re-upload and the BBC Radio 1 cover. Nosedive alone brought in 29% of the channel’s views in the last 30 days.

  3. 03

    The Goddamn, Lizard Man! visualiser is broken.

    It shows “Video unavailable” but still sits in Videos. Views stopped at 10,551 in late September. The replacement is audio only.

  4. 04

    Live is hard to find.

    The Live Performances playlist is the last section on Home, misses the BBC Radio 1 session, and four of its seven videos are other channels’. The Cannonball playlist isn’t in album order.

  5. 05

    Shorts don’t say what song they are.

    8 of 21 name a song. 14 have no description. Titles like “🫧” or “Baby get me back over you.” give a new viewer nowhere to go.

Method and gaps

Catalogue: every public upload from the YouTube Data API through our Watcher integration (46 rows, uncapped), read 20 Nov 2026 at 10:35 UTC. Format: a Short is a video on the channel’s Shorts tab (21). Maegan (Visualizer) is 53 seconds but horizontal, so it counts as long-form.

Watcher: 180 daily channel snapshots (25 May–20 Nov 2026) and per-video daily series. The channel counter updates every 1–4 days, so windows use the nearest stored day. Per-video series start 15 Sep, so earlier first-week figures are not available.

Coach: the original campaign record, created 18 May 2026 and last edited 29 May 2026. No earlier version is stored, so the plan shown is the 29 May version. The Coach's automatic matcher links both the Goddamn, Lizard Man! and Get Up and Dance moments to the Get Up and Dance visualiser; the deck maps them by hand.

Campaign window: Coach 20 Apr → 20 Nov. The cycle began earlier: Only Girl (Single Version), the album’s opening track, came out on 17 Nov 2025. Album day follows Bandcamp (7 Aug 2026); the album tracks went up at 23:00 UTC on 6 Aug, midnight UK time.

Lifetime views are totals at 20 Nov, not views earned inside the campaign. Window gains come from Watcher only.

The live show: Venue and date from the video's own description. Separate from the New York dates (Night Club 101, 29 Oct; Union Pool Brooklyn, 30 Oct), which appear only in fan-filmed uploads; no New York multicam footage was supplied.

Views read from public YouTube search and watch pages on 20 Nov 2026. These are other channels' uploads and are not owned-channel results. Not used: retention, click-through, geography, traffic sources or paid/organic splits. These need Studio.
