Lifetime channel counter

The 230 public uploads sum to 111,033,880. The lifetime counter reads 188,997,142. All format analysis in this deck uses the measurable 111.0M catalogue.

The difference may sit in removed or private videos, or in counting differences at YouTube's end. Public counters also move through the day — read at 15:20 UTC the catalogue summed to 111,033,880, earlier the same day 111,031,833 — which is why deck figures are rounded.

What the channel published each season

Season one: the official music video and five Shorts. Season two — the bigger chart year — two Shorts and no video asset. A December 2025 network television performance was not published to the channel; the catalogue shows no upload at all between 31 October 2025 and 3 February 2026. The one new Christmas asset since is the Annie DiRusso live version, published 14 August 2026.

Live performance

Twelve songs have both an official video and a live version. The official video leads in ten of them. The two exceptions:

Both are also the two lowest-viewed official music videos in the catalogue, so the deck's claim is narrow: live is a second opening for a song whose video didn't land.

Stone Pony: 91 minutes, 227,059 views, published 5 June 2026, never split into songs. Red Rocks, 8 July 2024: three songs from one night published individually — 171,061 + 143,285 + 68,986 = 383,332. The channel holds 28 single-song live videos in total.

Red Rocks is precedent, not a forecast. Stone Pony edits should be treated as a test, and remain subject to rights clearance.

Song support

Songs with an official video and a lyric video and an official audio and a live version: three — I Wanna Get Better (30.67M), Rollercoaster (18.92M), Alfie's Song (3.72M). The most recent was 2018.

Every total in the deck's support table counts those same four asset types and nothing else, which is why a song's figure there is lower than its all-upload total: the "you and forever" official video alone is 2,198,689 of its 2,750,183. Four lyric videos exist — I Wanna Get Better (3,714,016), Alfie's Song (563,167), Shadow (480,248), Rollercoaster (128,413). Shadow has a lyric video, an audio and a live version but no official music video, which is why it is not in the support table.

The older songs are also the channel's biggest, so this does not establish that fuller support caused their performance. The recommendation is therefore a test.

Shorts

94 Shorts · median 6,083 views. Two exceeded 50,000: Modern Girl 153K (billed as the only live performance of the song that exists) and Tiny Moves 145K (Margaret Qualley's choreography). The third best is 46,996.

Useful as a creative signal only: test distinctive moments rather than routine clipping.

Open questions we cannot answer from here

  1. Redistribution. Would individual Stone Pony songs add viewing, or redistribute it from the full concert?
  2. Seasonality. How does the Christmas video's YouTube curve behave through a complete November–January period?
  3. Studio behaviour. What do retention, traffic sources and audience data reveal that public data cannot?
