We set a plan.
Here’s how it played out.

Build · tease the story Done

The countdown landed on the day.

Dated countdown Shorts from 12 Aug resolved on 10 Sep. The same device ran again into the album: 12 Shorts in nine days.

Hero · define the moment Done

Three singles, three films.

My Whole World, Cold Blue Dawn and KOYSAR each got a distinct film. Together they hold 82.9% of public views on campaign uploads.

Extend · deepen the world Done

A live room, a visualiser, every track.

Two live-room performances, a visualiser for the lead single and a video for every album track on release day.

A second destination inside 7–14 days Partly

2 of 4 had another long-form upload within 14 days.

Cold Blue Dawn → My Whole World (Live), 8 days. Album → Rose (Live), 3 days. My Whole World → visualiser took 15; KOYSAR → album 21.

Give release week a centre Partly

Programmed across the week, with a focal point.

The plan said don’t publish everything at once. The album still landed in one day, but Rose (Live) three days later gave release week its focal point.

Always on · connect the eras Partly

Partly in place; connections need checking.

The O My Beloved playlist holds 5 videos, not the album, and the lead single’s description has no links. End screens, cards and Community posts weren’t checked.

The content plan happened. What’s left is the programming around it.

Methodology notes
Windows
Lead single to album + 5 days in each year, so release week is included in both. Album date: Friday 6 November 2026, per the band’s announcement (Radio X, 10 Sep 2026) and the channel’s release-day uploads. Tracklist: 13 tracks.
Follow-through
Days from a 2026 hero moment to the next long-form upload of any kind (not necessarily the same song). 2024 was not recalculated on this definition, so no cross-year rate is given. 2026 moments: My Whole World → visualiser 15; Cold Blue Dawn → My Whole World (Live) 8; KOYSAR → album 21; album → Rose (Live) 3.
Channel views
Daily rates are averaged between irregular Watcher readings of the public lifetime total. The release-day rate covers 6–9 November only. Release movement is not credited to any upload.
Subscribers
Public counts are rounded by YouTube, so movement is shown as readings, not a growth figure.
Comments
Qualitative, from relevance-ranked public samples. Personal stories in comments were read but are not quoted.
Observed vs recommended
Playlist contents, description links and the title typo were checked on 11 Nov. Slide 12’s suggestions are recommendations; whether more live-room footage exists is a question for the team.
Paid support
Approximate 2026 split of 60% paid / 40% organic, supplied by the campaign team; coverage (videos, period) to be confirmed. Not applied to all campaign uploads. 2024 split unknown, so no like-for-like organic comparison.
Not visible to us
Impressions, click-through, retention, traffic sources, end screens, cards and Community posts. Studio access would sharpen the read.
