07 · Lessons from Forever

What to keep. What to change.

Keep

  • Videos with a story and a face. Heavy with Hugo Weaving (426K) — the comments are about him.
  • The vocalists. Forever with BROODS was the era's biggest video (455K).
  • The full concert film. Live at Airfields (390K) is still being watched in 2026.

Change

  • One video per song. Separate audio and video uploads split each single's views — Lights Up 323K + 302K.
  • More for the album tracks. Six tracks went up as plain audio (34–91K). A Short or live clip each gives them a way in.
  • Shorts from day one. Five Shorts in 2021; now Shorts are where Crave You is being found.
  • Bring the live film closer. Airfields came 13 months after the album — inside the campaign it could carry the songs.
08 · From other electronic artists

Borrow one idea from each.

Peggy Gou

465K subscribers

Let the song live in the crowd first. Nanana had a karaoke video (8.1M) and crowd sing-along Shorts (0.2–1.3M) months before its official video.

For Flight FacilitiesPlay the lead single live and post the crowd moments before the video lands.

Fred again..

1.5M subscribers · larger

A named live-set series. Studio Live, Rooftop Live, USB — each full set an event. Rooftop Live, London: 31.7M.

For Flight FacilitiesDecades and Airfields already work this way. Make a full album set the era's centrepiece.

Eric Prydz

441K subscribers

A regular programme. EPIC Radio goes up every two weeks; each episode draws 0.9–1.6M.

For Flight FacilitiesThe Decade Mixes (up to 408K) are loved but rare. A lighter, regular mix keeps the channel moving between singles.
