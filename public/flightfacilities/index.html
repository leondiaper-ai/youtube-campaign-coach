How we read the comments

About 1,640 top-level comments — up to 40 per video, ordered by relevance — on 44 videos chosen to cover the classics, the Forever era, the edits, the mixes, the live uploads and the 2026 Shorts. Themes were counted by keyword and read by hand. A selected sample, so these are qualitative signals, not a measure of the audience.

  • Nostalgia and rediscovery — on 34 of 44 videos.
  • Discovery elsewhere — GTA V, Forza Horizon, The Sims, a Google Home ad, a Shrek/Squidward meme, a sample by Bryson Tiller.
  • Vocalists — Reggie Watts and Owl Eyes most often; Emma Louise.
  • Decade Mixes — requests for the next one; fan-made tracklists.
  • Edits — requests for streaming and vinyl releases.
  • Rare — full-show requests (4), how songs were made, song-ID questions.
