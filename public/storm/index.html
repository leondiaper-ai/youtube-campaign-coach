What is deliberately not claimed

That YouTube caused the downstream streaming growth. Video has repeatedly been at the centre of STORM's biggest discovery moments; that is the claim and it is as far as the evidence goes.

That Asian YouTube demand is failing to convert. Platform adoption differs substantially by market, so this is an open question rather than a diagnosis.

That YouTube can replace the remaining active-stream shortfall. Nothing here supports it.

No cost-per-view to stream conversion modelling, and no forecasts. Scorecard targets are proposed tests.

Measurement notes

Views are plays, not people. No YouTube Studio data is used anywhere here: no impressions, click-through rate, retention, watch time or traffic sources.

Comment theme percentages are share of coded comments and sum to more than 100 because a comment can carry several themes.

Shorts and long-form views are not the same unit.
