The strategy
Three connected priorities

Video created the moment. YouTube can help extend it.

01

Create more
discovery

Shorts built from the strongest existing moments. Destinations for what audiences are already searching. Content that gives recommendations something to surface. Cross-channel participation and distribution beyond the owned channel.

02

Build the
visual world

The dancers, the choreography, the live performance, the filmmaking and the BTS — the parts of STORM the audience is already naming in its own words.

03

Turn attention
into audience

Connect every entrance back to the film, the channel, the song on YouTube Music and the wider GENER8ION catalogue, so a view becomes a return rather than a single play.

The question isn't whether STORM can find an audience. It already has. It's how much of that attention we can keep.

How we'd judge it
Discovery

Are new pieces reaching people the film doesn't? Views and velocity, Shorts viewed against swiped, new viewers.

Depth

Are viewers consuming more of the world? Supporting-content share, returning viewers, end-screen and related traffic.

Audience

Is demand becoming an audience? Subscribers per 1,000 views, returning viewers, wider catalogue consumption.

Sustained demand

Does the cycle extend? Hero velocity, the viewing baseline, territory performance, YouTube Music where available.
