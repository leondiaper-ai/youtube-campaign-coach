Where the findings come from.

Public YouTube data captured on 17 November 2026. The reviewed upload window is 17 May–17 November 2026. The snapshot is dated and does not auto-refresh. Headline totals cover the retrieved artist uploads, not the full release catalogue or videos hosted by other channels.

The initial conclusion

Both channels have very little recent upload activity. Particular uploads attract views, likes and comments. That supports an offer to help the artists do more on their channels. It does not establish why subscriber totals differ from other platforms, or prove poor subscriber conversion.

Comments: what this review does and does not say

These are API-reported comment counts. We have not read the comment text or assessed sentiment, recurring names or fan relationships. Comments support a finding of participation, not a claim about positive sentiment or loyalty. Like/comment fields may be absent or unavailable and the current collector coerces missing values to zero.
