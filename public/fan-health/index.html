THE DATA BEHIND THE READ

Activity. Engagement.
Where the findings come from.

Public YouTube data captured on 17 November 2026. Window: 17 May–17 November. Artist videos / Shorts, release audio and partner uploads are shown separately. These are dated readings, not live growth figures.

The initial conclusion

Both artists have recent music releases, but little artist video or Shorts activity. MPH has a clear response to performance content, on its own channel and Mixmag. Interplanetary Criminal’s audio uploads attract views and likes, with almost no Shorts activity. The opportunity is more artist content around music that is already reaching listeners. These public figures do not explain the gap with Instagram/TikTok followers or measure subscriber conversion.

Comments: what this review does and does not say

These are API-reported comment counts. We have not read the comment text or assessed sentiment, recurring names or fan relationships. Comments support a finding of participation, not a claim about positive sentiment or loyalty. Like/comment fields may be absent or unavailable and the current collector coerces missing values to zero.

Shorts: checked on YouTube itself

On 17 November 2026, the public MPH Shorts tab showed two Shorts: Pick your favorite track (84 seconds) and Tear the club up (36 seconds). Both fall inside this audit period. The Interplanetary Criminal Shorts tab showed one: Slow Burner summer is here (47 seconds), dated 27 June 2025. That is one Short on the channel but zero new Shorts in this six-month period. These are visible public Shorts at capture, not a record of deleted/private uploads. This direct check corrects the collector’s duration-only rule, which missed the 84-second MPH Short.

MPH: performance also connects on Mixmag

MPH | Mixmag Lab London (29 October): 144,232 views · 2,909 likes · 261 comments. Four associated clips add 41,013 views. This is partner-channel reach, separate from MPH’s own uploads. It supports testing more performance-led content; it does not prove those viewers subscribe to MPH.

Why the Watcher flags MPH

247 comments ÷ 117,112 views × 1,000 = 2.11 comments per 1,000 views. The Watcher’s fixed roster reference is 0.65 (74 channels, measured 11 October): 2.11 ÷ 0.65 = 3.2×. Counts are lifetime on the retrieved uploads, not comments posted in 90 days. Different upload ages limit the comparison. We do not apply this above-baseline claim to Interplanetary Criminal.
