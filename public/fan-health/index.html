Comments: what this review does and does not say

These are API-reported comment counts. We have not read the comment text or assessed sentiment, recurring names or fan relationships. Comments support a finding of participation, not a claim about positive sentiment or loyalty. Like/comment fields may be absent or unavailable and the current collector coerces missing values to zero.

Shorts: a classification limitation

The existing collector labels uploads of 62 seconds or less as Shorts. Duration alone cannot establish YouTube’s actual format. MPH’s 36-second and 84-second clips need a surface check before stating an exact Shorts count. Interplanetary Criminal’s only returned short-duration upload is 47 seconds, dated 27 June 2025, outside the audit. No short-duration uploads were returned within its six-month window.

Growth and cross-platform comparisons

Public subscribers are rounded. Interplanetary Criminal’s Watcher response has one day of history and null 7-day view and 7/30-day subscriber changes. The mph slug did not resolve in artist-live. That does not rule out MPH history elsewhere. We do not report growth, returning viewers, retention, subscriber attribution or loyalty. Instagram/TikTok follower totals and activity have not been independently verified.

Channel totals do not reconcile with the upload inventory

MPH’s endpoint reports 8,942,964 lifetime views and four public videos. The four returned uploads total 117,112 views. Interplanetary Criminal’s endpoint reports 6,122,284 lifetime views and three public videos, while its playlist returns 13 upload records. Some uploads predate the reported channel creation. The cause remains unresolved. We exclude lifetime views from the headline and base the findings on individually returned upload records. Deleted, private, Topic, partner and fan uploads are not a verified complete inventory here.

The existing full-catalogue route resolved each channel, paged its uploads playlist and hydrated video statistics through the YouTube Data API. Neither pull hit its cap.
