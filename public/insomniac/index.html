How the channel has evolved

Trailers built the channel. Today the sets do the heavy lifting.

Share of all 194.2M views, by format · lifetime
The main EDC Las Vegas trailer, edition by edition · views to date

For a decade the EDC trailer was the channel's biggest event of the year: 1.6–3.6M views for every edition to 2020. Since 2022 it has settled at 110–200K — and even the 2023 trailer, with three years behind it, sits at 165K, so this is more than older videos having longer to collect views. It shows a change in the trailer's role on YouTube, not a verdict on the marketing: public data can't show where trailer audiences watch now. In 2026 the audience came for the sets.

Festival content across the calendar · 2026

EDC gets the full cycle. The rest of the calendar is the opportunity.

EDC Las Vegas ran the whole cycle on YouTube: a lineup-reveal stream in February (227K), the trailer, the live festival, then 79 sets. Zombie Apocalypse added five. Twelve other 2026 events — EDC Mexico, Beyond Wonderland, Escape and more — appear as a trailer or an aftermovie. Whether they were filmed, and on what terms, isn't visible from outside.

The autumn is quieter: 19 uploads in September–October, against 135 a year earlier. Nearly all of the difference is Shorts (115 then, 11 now); the year-long high-volume Shorts run has ended, and output is back near 2023–24 levels. That is a publishing rhythm, not a measure of demand — and Insomniac TV has kept the channel live throughout.

Beyond the festival · YouTube's native tools

Building a year-round YouTube destination.

Live
01 · Always-on stations

Develop Insomniac TV into a programmed destination

Already: live around the clock since 20 May, top of Home, chat on, playing sets "from festivals like EDC Las Vegas, Zombie Apocalypse, and more". An 842-video Insomniac TV playlist holds the archive.

Next: themed blocks — a trance weekend, a techno night, artist hours — and countdown programming before each major event.

Insomniac TV ↗ Reference: Metallica TV ↗

Metallica TV, live since March 2026, mixes music videos, live cuts and full concerts across eras, names what's playing, and keeps live chat open.

02 · Afterparties

Build on something that already works

Already: EDC Afters Live with Kaskade (64 min) and with Armin van Buuren (59 min), EDC weekend 2026.

Next: make it a fixture at two or three more flagships, and around exclusive artist moments — not every event.

Afterparties are Premium-exclusive live streams with artists (YouTube Help); confirm availability per event with the YouTube partner team.

03 · Premieres, Live Redirect, connected viewing

Turn single uploads into fan moments

Already: Premieres for the EDC trailer, the Odd Mob release (667K, the year's biggest upload outside the sets) and 2 of the 79 sets; Collaboration credits on 61 of 63.

Next: premiere a few headline sets with a countdown; Live Redirect from a live stream into a set Premiere or an artist's own stream; Shorts linked to their full set.

Live Redirect: 1,000+ subscribers, no strikes, and the destination channel's permission (YouTube Help). Link and end-screen use isn't public.

Insomniac already has the content, the artists and the live-event expertise. The next opportunity is connecting those experiences into a stronger year-round YouTube destination.
