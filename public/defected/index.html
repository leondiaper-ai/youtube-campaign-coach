The hinge

What to protect.

01

The Friday show

52 a year since 2019. The channel's only weekly appointment, with a sleeve system that reads at grid size. Keep the slot, keep the colours, give it a home (slide 8).

02

The DFTD sleeve

Three million-view records in 2025. A packaging system that says “Defected release” before the title is read. Extend it to every release upload, not most.

03

The HQ engine

59 sets, nine over 100K. The studio series kept the sets going between seasons and produced more big ones than any venue. Keep it running; settle its title order (slide 9).

04

The mixed archive

At least four in ten pass 100K, every year since 2022. The compilation is the cheapest large audience the channel has. Put it back on a calendar.

None of the gaps that follow are production gaps. They are routing, packaging and cadence — fixable with what the label already owns.

  • Story of the Sample · 30 seconds of music history → the classic's upload

A test worth running · 2

Season two: cut it before you post it.

Jan 2024Moments in Music, season one · nine episodes in 25 monthsFeb 2026

Four episodes in the first ten weeks, then gaps of three, seven, seven and five months. Lifetime views 2.4K–31K, median 17K. Every episode had an announcement Short; almost none had an excerpt. The one story clip cut from a conversation — Skream, “the track that started it all” — did 14.3K, more than any announcement. The series already has what the radio show lacks: a branded cover, a proper playlist, a Home shelf.

The label's planned season two is not yet announced publicly, so no launch date is stated here. The ask is about how it is cut and released, whatever the date.
14.3KSkream · the track that started it all
  1. Three excerpt Shorts per episode, cut from the conversation, each linked to the episode. The Skream clip is the template; the announcement Short becomes the fourth, not the only.
  2. A fixed fortnightly slot for the season, published as a run, with the season playlist and the Podcasts tab updated on day one.
  3. Episode art that carries the guest's face and one line — the S1E01 sleeve already does this — plus chapters in every description.
  4. Route the guest's music. Each episode's end screen and description point to that guest's Defected releases or set; each of those points back.
Measure: episode views at 28 days against the season-one median (17K); excerpt-Short median against the announcement-Short median (about 4K); related-link click-through in Studio.

Method

Sources
Public YouTube Data API v3 through the project's own routes (uploads, playlists, channel sections); the live channel in a browser on 16 October 2026; Apple Podcasts and Spotify for the podcast listing. No YouTube Studio data of any kind.
Comparison rule
No lifetime-view ranking across upload ages. Series are compared within the same window or by structural measures: cadence, consistency, format mix, share passing a threshold. Where a comparison cannot be made fairly the figures are stated with their limits.
Attribution
Public data shows co-occurrence. No result is attributed to a setting, a title, a thumbnail or a host. Shorts-to-long-form conversion is a hypothesis for the test on slide 10.
Snapshots
The channel entered daily public snapshots on 16 October 2026. Until there is history, nothing here describes momentum, release-week shape or catalogue growth. The follow-up plan is on slide 12.
Not retrieved
The 243 oldest uploads (2009 to mid-2011); the Live and Posts tabs; end screens, cards and Community posts, which the public API does not expose.
