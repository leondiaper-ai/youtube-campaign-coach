Appendix · method, sources and limits

What this is built on.

Channel data

YouTube Data API v3 — the complete public uploads playlist, 198 of 198 videos, uncapped, read 7 October 2026 from @DBlockEuropeOfficial (UCzyLniLK2YQ5xCJigB2WetA).

Lifetime 972,502,684 views · 661,000 subscribers · 198 public uploads. The 198 public videos sum to 498,116,125 views.

Per-video daily observation series for velocity; the series begins mid-September 2026.

VMG UK Landscape — Chartmetric 25 Sep 2026, VMG UK consumption 23 Mar–20 Sep 2026.

Not available

No YouTube Studio data anywhere: no impressions, click-through rate, retention, watch time, traffic sources, unique viewers or revenue.

Views are plays, not people. No figure here counts humans reached.

Subscriber counts are rounded by YouTube to three significant figures.

Shorts and long-form views are not the same unit — YouTube changed Shorts counting in March 2025.

Velocity exists only for videos with an observation series. Overseas and the other large catalogue records have none, so no velocity is claimed for them.

How the audit was built

Every upload was classified from its title and duration: official video, visualiser, lyric, live/performance, BTS/vlog/documentary, interview, trailer, remix, audio, Short (≤62s), or other.

Assets were matched back to the record they support by song name, which is how the gap map and the 248.7M figure are derived.

"Missing" means no such asset exists on this channel. It is not a claim that no footage exists, that nothing was ever made, or that anyone did anything wrong.

Release-day layers are days with 3+ uploads, from publish timestamps, stated as counts only.

Research sources

"4 out of 5 music superfans use YouTube to engage with music content and/or artists, more than any other online platform" — Luminate, cited by YouTube for Artists, artists.youtube/resources/growing-your-fanbase/. YouTube names no report, date or sample for the Luminate study; it is presented here as YouTube's published claim.

Laufey 80% returning viewers and the "visual storytelling videos every few weeks" guidance — YouTube for Artists, Post-Release Strategy, internal YouTube data 22–28 Apr 2026.

Top Fan Exclusive 10× comments per view — YouTube for Artists, internal data Dec 2025–Mar 2026.

73% / 72% — YouTube Culture & Trends, In Search of Mainstream, with NRG, US, Apr 2026, n=939, ages 14–44. This is a general-population figure, not a music-fan figure, and is labelled as such on the slide.

Open questions

The channel reports 972,502,684 lifetime views; the 198 current public videos sum to 498,116,125. The difference of 474,386,559 is unexplained. It may include deleted, private or unlisted videos and accounting changes. It is not evidence that those views sit on another channel, and no part of this deck treats it as such.

GRM Daily hosts early DBE material. Its scale could not be measured — their channel exceeds the retrieval cap — so no figure is claimed.

The Dave comparison is one dataset on one date. It is not an efficiency score and subscribers are not claimed to cause views.

No claim is made that a past decision would have produced more views. Every recommendation is a bet, not a forecast.
