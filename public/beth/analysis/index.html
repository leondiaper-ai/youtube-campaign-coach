Evidence behind the recommendations

ActionEvidenceRead
Give Act I a door 2 uploads in the new era · Pretty Villain 1st of 26 on comment rate Strong current signal
Put the audience back in the work Response uploads + Flowers = 82.9% of all channel views Strong structural signal
Capture live as songs 0 standalone live destinations · the one full show did 6,651 Worth testing
Finish something small Two announced series, 1 of 5 and 2 of 5 delivered Observed pattern, twice

The response share is a statement about where views landed, not about what caused them. Three of the four response uploads went up within eleven weeks of each other during a single period of attention, so the format and the moment cannot be separated from outside.

Open questions we cannot answer from here

  1. Where the social audience goes. The brief cites a 4.3M-view social clip. We can see that no equivalent exists on YouTube, but not whether that audience would follow if one did.
  2. Whether the 2021 reach is reachable again. Response songs worked once, inside one window, under distribution conditions we cannot observe or reproduce.
  3. Studio behaviour. What retention, traffic sources and audience data reveal that public counts cannot — including whether the high comment rate on recent uploads reflects a smaller, more committed audience or a different traffic mix.
