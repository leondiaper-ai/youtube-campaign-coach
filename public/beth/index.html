Before recommending a three-act structure

neither.

"The first of 5 live sessions that will be uploaded every Friday over the next 5 weeks."

1 of 5 was ever published
2023 · a five-part series

What Do You Call It? is "episode 1". She's Pretty is "episode 2" — of "a five part series where we see Beth navigate a night out".

2 of 5 were ever published
Both quoted from the uploads' own descriptions · episodes 3, 4 and 5 do not exist; the next music video, fourteen months later, is not part of the series
The useful reading is not "they fail at series". Episode 2 is a 292,741-view music video and the lyric video before it did 265,963 — the series was working. What the channel has never had is a structure small enough to finish. Three acts is a better bet than five episodes only if each act's commitment is sized to what two people can actually deliver between four singles in ten weeks.
