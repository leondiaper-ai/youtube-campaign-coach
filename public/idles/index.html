YouTube guidance behind the recommendations

  • Channel optimisation: align the homepage, featured content and playlists with the release priority.
  • Pre-release, release day and post-release: prepare assets, connect formats and continue useful support.
  • Related Video for Shorts: eligible public or unlisted content on the same channel; advanced feature access required. Use a playlist to bring in partner-owned performances.
  • IDLES releases: release-date context.

What the label should check

  • Which songs and creative priorities will lead the next album?
  • What usable rehearsal, studio, tour or archive footage already exists, with rights cleared?
  • Who owns channel preparation, playlists, uploads and fan replies?
  • What do Studio watch time, returning viewers and traffic sources show after release week?

Recommendations are proposals. Budget, band availability, footage rights and release timing were not supplied. Ezra is a reference for clear storytelling, not an IDLES performance benchmark.
