And it was not "Coachella footage" that travelled. The channel published nine other Coachella Shorts that April; they did 231 to 1,530 views each, at or below the channel median. One specific post did 75,826. Directional, not proven — but it argues against clipping a tour and for deciding in advance what is actually distinctive.

The Short added meaningful incremental reach — ten times the median Short. The BTS added 727 views, which is 1.4% of the song's total. Neither is a verdict on the format. The point is that a support asset should be made because it has a job, not because the campaign template says there should be one.

The next 30–60 days

Don't support the album
evenly. Follow the signals.

Now

Name the two or three songs actually showing something

On current public figures, Wishing Bone has gathered 56,854 views in sixteen days — the fastest accumulation of any Amulet asset. Serpent is one day old with the highest like rate of the campaign. If I Could is the quietest of the three singles. That is a starting read, not a ranking.

7–14 days

Give the strongest song another destination

A live version, a second visual, a performance — something that gives people arriving at Wishing Bone somewhere else to go. Long-form is where the viewing is, so the next meaningful asset should be long-form.

30 days

Capture distinctive live moments, not generic clips

The North American run starts 23 October. Coachella showed a live moment can create outsized discovery — but it was distinctive, not just footage. Look for crowd interaction, a song visibly taking off live, backstage personality, tour milestones. Decide in advance what is worth capturing.

30–60 days

Let behaviour choose the next investment

By late November there will be real signal on which Amulet songs people return to. Put the next layer of production behind those, and accept that some album tracks will not get a campaign. That is the point.

Accumulation here is measured as views since publish divided by days since publish, which is a lifetime average rather than current velocity — a one-day-old asset's figure is close to noise. It is enough to say which songs got where they are quickly, not enough to forecast.
