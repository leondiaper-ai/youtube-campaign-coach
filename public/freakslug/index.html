The post-release lull is not a hypothesis on this channel — it has happened after both bodies of work, and the second time it ran for 244 days. It is also the one problem with a proven solution already in the record: when Freak Slug is on a stage, the channel moves.

What happens after the album cycle

North America starts 23 October.
That is the next six months.

The tour is not a promotional afterthought here — on the evidence it is the strongest content engine the channel has. One festival appearance generated a quarter of every Short view in the catalogue. A full North American run is forty-odd nights of the same raw material.

The point of dating this is the December problem. Last cycle the channel went from seventeen uploads in a month to one in four. The tour covers October and November; the decision that matters is what is already shot and scheduled for the weeks after it ends.

What we recommend

Four things, starting
with the tour.

01
Starts 23 October

Make the tour the publishing plan

A live video per leg and one moment Short a week, for as long as the run lasts. Not a highlights reel at the end — the channel's two previous restarts both came from a stage, and this is the longest stage time Freak Slug has had.

Coachella: 75,826 views — 26% of all Shorts views ever
02

Give the album's remaining songs their own assets

Serpent at D+13 was the right instinct and it is already the thing that separates this cycle from the last one. Keep going — a song video, a live version, a piece of behind-the-scenes. The record should still be releasing itself in January.

Girl, Intentions got three assets and outperformed every other song
03

Cut the Shorts count, raise the Shorts bar

Thirty-nine in the window, thirty-eight of them averaging 764 views. The ones that travel document something that happened. One good moment a week will reach more people than five restatements of the single, and it costs less to make.

2 of 163 Shorts have passed 10,000 views
04

Point something at FRIDAY

A 2020 upload is still the biggest thing on the channel at 614,437 views and nothing currently connects it to Amulet. End screens, a pinned comment, a playlist that runs it into the new record — the cheapest available audience is the one already arriving.
