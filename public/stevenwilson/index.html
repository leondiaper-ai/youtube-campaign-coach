More from the village hall.

Another album song, played by the three people on the session — the format fans responded to most this campaign.

Third priority · the visual world

A visualiser that takes fans further into the village.

What it gives the fan

The words, and more of the world, for a song without a film — around release day, when the whole album arrives.

Build it from the village's own material

The parish notices, the observances, the hall and the churchyard. Fans already followed the website into the comments.

Fans were invited into the artwork

In the spring, Steven asked fans for amateur photos of village life for the special edition. Where permissions allow, images like these could appear in a visualiser — and fans would recognise their own village in it.

Keep the making-of light

Video BTS has drawn modest audiences on this channel. A short piece with Mike Bennion on the wedding and the graveyard would answer the questions fans are asking without a full documentary.

Refuge (Lyric Video) 2.5M (2017) and Man Of The People (Official Lyric Video) 352K (2021), public views 20 Oct 2026 · photo call-out: channel post, spring 2026 (deadline 3 April), 1.2K likes · use of fan photos depends on the terms given at submission.

YouTube features referenced, and their status

  • Afterparty — "Premium-exclusive live streams with artists who host a video feed and live chat", after a Premiere or live stream; available where Premium launched. To set one up: "reach out to your YouTube partner representative". Help · YouTube for Artists. Confirm with partner manager.
  • Top Fans exclusive videos — choose "Top Fans" under Visibility at upload; "only available to artists with a minimum number of top fans". Release Strategy · Music Fandom. Trade reports describe it as rolling out in testing (May–June 2026). Confirm availability for the channel.
  • Stations — a continuous stream built from a channel's own videos, with live chat. No YouTube help page found; trade reports (Music Ally, RouteNote) say YouTube extended the test to "select new music artists" on 21–24 Aug 2026, by invitation. Confirm with partner manager.
  • Community posts — photos, GIFs, links, polls; replying to a comment with a Short is documented. Music Fandom. The channel has posts enabled.
  • Collaborations — up to 10 creators credited on a video. Help.
  • Premieres — both singles were Premieres (scheduled 10:00 UTC).
