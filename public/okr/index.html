More on formats, uploads and attention
Assets used · long-form and Shorts
Long-formShorts

27 uploads in 2024 (18 long-form, 9 Shorts) against 69 in 2026 (24 long-form, 45 Shorts): music videos, visualisers, a rehearsal film, three city films and festival Shorts, all inside one creative world.

Where the attention went · share of campaign-window views

In 2024 one video took 62.8% of the window and the top three took 84.7%. In 2026 the leader took 27.4% and the top three 56.5% — a music video, a second music video and a fourteen-minute rehearsal film. The campaign stopped depending on one upload.

Two jobs · 2026 window views by format
Long-form · 24 uploadsShorts · 45 uploads

Long-form carried 83.6% of window views from a third of the uploads; the 45 Shorts kept the channel present every week. Different jobs, judged separately.

Possible goals, measures and territory scope
Teams · possible goal
Bring more teams in

Build on US and LATAM interest. Ask every key territory: does the dashboard help, how is it used, and what is missing?

Track: teams actively using it, feedback and examples of decisions it helped.

Territories · possible goal
Know the biggest local channels

Proposed scope: map the our top 100 channels by local YouTube consumption in each key territory. Identify relevant VMG repertoire, then agree a smaller support list locally.

Track: territories mapped and priority channels agreed.

Labels · possible goal
Test what partners find useful

Partisan already uses and requests deep dives, and wants a dashboard view. Gather feedback with them and other interested labels on reviews and label-only views.

Track: partner feedback, repeat requests and ideas taken forward.

The top 100 is a proposed view of our channels in each territory, not a list of 100 artists to support. Local teams would select a smaller focus list based on repertoire, relationships and ability to act. Build on YouTube’s annual UK mapping request; agree data coverage and definitions with Data/Insights before extending it.
