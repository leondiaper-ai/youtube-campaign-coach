05 / Strategy

WHAT PARTISAN
DOES NEXT

01 / Confirm

MAP THE ASSETS

Check what has been filmed, approved and scheduled. Identify the strongest existing songs and any missing support formats before commissioning anything new.

02 / Programme

BUILD THE NEXT WINDOW

Use the best-supported songs as anchors, then surround them with live moments, lyric/visual support, Shorts and community activity. Do not assume unapproved assets exist.

03 / Measure

CHECK THE RESPONSE

Monitor the same upload cohort and Watcher channel movement weekly. Separate public asset viewing from total channel growth, and avoid claiming organic impact.
