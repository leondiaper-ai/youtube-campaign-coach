Sources and measurement

Capture dates. Catalogue counters and album review remain at 17 Nov 2026. Broadcast metadata, arrangement credits and comments were checked 18 Nov. No one-day growth is inferred from the two captures.

Views. Lifetime counters at capture, not views earned in historical campaign windows. No paid/organic split, CTR, retention, unique viewers or conversion is available. Cross-format and cross-era figures are descriptive; age, spend and selection differ.

Shorts¹. Existing API proxy: duration >0 and ≤62 seconds. Not verified Shorts-feed status; longer Shorts may be missed.

Watcher. 22 channel observations from 27 Oct; repeated counters and no release-week or previous-album baseline. No current growth-rate claim. Stone Fruit has a usable limited observed interval, not evidence it is the fastest current song.

Premieres. The app identifies a Premiere from scheduled and actual starts on an official music video. Both establish an aired broadcast; missing retained metadata is unknown. The producer conversation is a separate livestream, supported by its description. Its actual start is 16 May even though its publication date is 17 May.

Comments. 2,076 relevance-ranked top-level comments across 62 selected uploads (54 full-length and eight highest-viewed Shorts), max 100 each. This is not all commenters or a representative audience. Quotes and like counts are recorded in the signals file; no sentiment score or inferred demand total is used.

Partner footage. KEXP full performance is linked on the partner channel: watch the session ↗. BBC and Berlin examples are artist-channel excerpts. Availability of full takes, rights and permission to duplicate partner uploads must be checked.

Post-album proposal. Eight weeks, 23 Nov–17 Jan: 16 proposed Shorts, one proposed full performance and one optional second full-length version. Asset delivery and approval are unconfirmed; no view forecast is made. Read the proposed schedule ↗.
