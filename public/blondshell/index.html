Appendix / Data integrity

How this
audit was built.

The same public-API and Watcher evidence rules used for the Ezra Collective and Kings of Leon reviews.

Data coverage

Checking catalogue completeness and snapshot coverage.

Public data limits

There are no YouTube Studio metrics: no impressions, CTR, retention, uniques or traffic sources. Public views do not distinguish paid from organic. Channel movement co-occurring with a release cannot be attributed to an individual asset.

Campaign classification

Album windows and song title matches are working classifications. The 2025 comparison uses a provisional calendar window and is not an age-matched performance test. Short-form classification by duration is approximate. Before sharing externally, manually confirm each relevant upload, the album announcement date and release-day chronology.
