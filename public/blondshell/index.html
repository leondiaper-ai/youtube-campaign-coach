Format definitions, calculations and exclusions

Short clip is the existing full-catalogue duration proxy: >0 and ≤62 seconds. This does not establish vertical aspect ratio, native Shorts status or Shorts-feed consumption, and can miss longer Shorts. All 99 current-window clips use that proxy. Full-song formats were reviewed by title and description opening, against the official tracklist. Music videos: Violins and Stone Fruit. Visualizer: Heart Has To Work So Hard. Lyric: Fur Elise. Seven album-day official audios. The short announcing Fur Elise calls it a visualizer; the primary upload and Partisan label it a lyric video, which is the classification used here.

Playable current-window totals: 110 uploads, 675,544 lifetime views. Album full songs: 11, 396,051 views (58.62697% of window-row views). Surrounding clips: 99, 279,493 views (90% of uploads; median 2,321). Music videos: 236,044 (59.599% of album-video views); visualizer 98,977; lyric 33,902; audios 27,128. Percentages describe the captured cohorts and can change as videos age. They are not age-independent performance benchmarks.

71 uploads before release day; 15 on release day (8 full songs + 7 clips); 24 after release day (all clips). Release-week window 25 Sep–1 Oct: 18 playable uploads (8 full songs + 10 clips). None of these present lifetime asset counters as views generated during release week.

One Live Stream row, oHAC9CnFgmE, has duration 0, views 0 and no actualStart or scheduledStart. It is retained in raw evidence and excluded from playable upload counts. It is not evidence of a completed stream or Premiere. The catalogue is complete as returned by the API, not a census of partner/fan/Topic viewing.

Active-week counts use seven-day bins anchored to each matched start, with a final two-day bin.
