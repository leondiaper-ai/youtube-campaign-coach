On release weekend alone it added +582 and +581 followers on consecutive days — 14× an ordinary day. No release since has produced anything like it.

What was different — and what we are not claiming

It had an official video, a release weekend, and a band returning after a quiet 2025. We cannot prove those caused the result. We can say this is the one release configuration worth understanding, because nothing since has matched its conversion.

What this means

Editorial support is not the variable that decides the outcome. Motherlands had almost none and outperformed; Defibrillator had the most and didn't transform; In Motion had a full asset set and moved nothing.

The album opportunity

The challenge for 23 October is not more reach for one song. It is whether album week can turn PTS's attention into people who go deeper — into the album, the artist profile, and a durable follow.

Creative implication

The sharpest reactions come from things that are human, specific and culturally rooted rather than generic release assets. Treat that as a direction for album-week content, not as proof of what drives performance.

For Commercial

DSP support and audience response are not telling the same story.

Two questions for Commercial

Why did Motherlands outperform its Spotify support — and can PTS's strong support plus building attention be converted into album intent before the 23rd?

Positions are indicative. Playlist reach is a current reading and favours recent releases, so support is not a like-for-like of support at release.

18 days to album day

Four decisions before 23 October.

01 · Hold

Don't spend against PTS yet

Organic YouTube velocity is still healthy at day 10, and the lift is video-specific. Spending now buys views it is getting free.

Reconsider if daily gain < ~3,000 for three consecutive days
02 · Investigate

Take Motherlands back to Spotify

Weakest distribution of the campaign, second-best listener week. Costs a conversation, not a budget.

Take the counter-evidence in: its YouTube asset is the campaign's weakest
03 · Design

Build album week as one moment, not a schedule

The only times this campaign moved more than one platform, several things landed inside the same few days. Four singles released alone moved almost nothing.

A campaign hypothesis — the evidence is a pattern, not proven causality
04 · Fix, then test

The podcast has no route on it at all

18,101 views, the campaign's best engagement, our one proven reach into non-fans — and zero links in the description. It is 8× larger than any other asset missing a route.

Fix it this week · then measure follower and subscriber gain per 1,000 reached, not streams
