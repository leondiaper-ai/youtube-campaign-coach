Appendix B · what we are not claiming

The limits this deck is built inside.

Corrected during the audit
  • A track from another artist was used as a negative benchmark in an earlier Spotify rule. Verified absent from all 310 YouTube uploads and all 98 Chartmetric tracks. Removed, and the rule rebuilt.
  • A "2× median day" paid threshold was never derived from Snuts data. Removed.
  • "Follower gain per 1,000 reached vs 41/day" compared a rate per impression with a rate per day. Removed.
  • Motherlands was called a conversion success. It converted at 1.34× median. Reframed as listener movement above observable support.
Still unknown
  • PTS traffic source. The acceleration is verified; the mechanism is not. No claim is made about Browse, Suggested or Search.
  • Save rate, SPL and algorithmic share for four of five releases.
  • Any paid activity on any release — so nothing here separates organic from bought.
  • Territory movement. Chartmetric gives a current city snapshot and no history.
  • Daily Spotify listener resolution. Sunday averages 3.95× Thursday all year, so listeners are read weekly only.
