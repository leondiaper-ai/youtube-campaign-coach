Who Tom is on YouTube

A look and a sound you can spot in a second.

A songwriter from Sunderland on Fiction Records, making dance-punk you can move to. Polka dots and braces, camcorder footage, and visuals made with Katie Anderson. The latest EP builds a whole world: Tommy's Dance Place, a club where "everyone is invited".

What it means

The identity is already there. The opportunity is to put it in front of people more often — and make each piece easy to follow.

Ideas from artists at a similar stage

One idea to borrow from each.

Antony Szmierek

5.5K subscribers · Manchester

Keep going after the album. Flight Simulator (feat. Craig Cash) arrived with his second album and added 21K views in the five weeks after it. Each album also has its own playlist.

Doesn't tell us the cameo caused the views.

mary in the junkyard

7.9K subscribers · London

A simple live version of a single. Crash Landing's live session recording sits next to its video and is still adding about 2K views a month.

Doesn't mean every single needs a session.

Jamie Webster

57K subscribers · Liverpool · larger

Film the big hometown show. Live From Sefton Park gave him several songs to release over months — Weekend In Paradise is at 312K.

A much bigger audience; it's the idea, not the scale.

Paid support — a neutral question

We don't know what paid support any video received. Like rates vary for many reasons (placement, audience, format) and don't establish paid activity. Asking the label what support Put On A Record Tommy and I Don't Blame You had lets us read their results in context. Studio's traffic sources would show "YouTube advertising" alongside organic sources.

Comments (qualitative)

~450 top-level comments across 15 videos, ordered by relevance. Themes appear across several videos; quotes illustrate them. Likes on a comment are not a measure of agreement.

  • Dancing — Put On A Record Tommy, I Don't Blame You, Our Song, Fashion and the Shorts.

  • Familiar echoes — Talking Heads, Bowie, Jagger, Mark E. Smith, Depeche Mode, early MTV. Several commenters describe themselves as older listeners. That's a recurring voice in the comments, not a measure of the audience's age, which only Studio could show.

  • Isolated — a few notes on live sound; one "lacks originality".

Basics — what the data supports

  • Descriptions (verified). Full descriptions came back for all 106 uploads. 5 contain a link (2021–23). 68 are empty, mostly live clips and Shorts. The singles have full credits but no listen, ticket or mailing-list links.
  • Captions (needs Studio). The API's caption flag is false on every upload. That indicates no caption tracks are listed; YouTube's automatic captions aren't reliably reflected there.
  • Playlists (verified). Lower Third (6), Sky Isle of Wight (3), Tom on Telly (6), Best of Me (16), Piano Sessions (6), Scruff Of The Neck TV (4). The Releases tab lists the EPs automatically.
  • Official Artist Channel. A Releases tab and a merged Music videos shelf suggest the channel is an OAC; confirm in Studio.
  • Not visible publicly: end screens, cards, pinned comments, Shorts related-video links.
  • Community. The last post was about five months ago (summer show pre-sale via mailing list and WhatsApp).
