01 · Who Tom is on YouTube

There's already something distinctive here.

  • A look you spot in a second. Polka dots, braces, camcorder texture, bold title cards — mostly made with Katie Anderson.
  • Performance at the centre. Nearly every official video is Tom singing and moving to camera, not an abstract visualiser.
  • A world. The latest EP built a club, Tommy's Dance Place, where "everyone is invited".
  • Fans talk about the dancing and the shows — "Impossible not to dance." · "Seen him three times, just gets better and better."
