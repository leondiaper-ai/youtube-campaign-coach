Reading the numbers fairly

  • No conversion rate. 6.8K is net subscribers gained on the channel; 9.1M is unique viewers across all channels. They have different scopes, so we don’t divide one by the other.
  • Viewers are not views, and returning viewers are not subscribers.
  • Total reach vs Videos report. Total reach (26.8M) includes other channels; the Videos report (16.2M) covers official videos only. They’re never combined.
  • Studio vs Watcher. The public lifetime counter rose from 150.4M (15 Sep) to 161.0M (14 Nov), about 10.6M in 60 days. Studio puts the official channel at roughly 14M over 90 days (53% of 26.8M). The two are similar in scale; the public counter updates in steps, so Studio is used for all period figures.
  • Lifetime is not campaign. Catalogue video totals span up to 17 years on YouTube. They show what lasts, not what a campaign did.
  • Visible uploads. The 70 public uploads carry 96.5M lifetime views against a channel total of 161.0M. The difference sits in videos that are no longer public, so concentration shares are of visible uploads.
