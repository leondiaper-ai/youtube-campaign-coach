The last act

VENUS GRRRLS have already run most of a campaign on YouTube. Three singles, sixteen uploads, and more than a third of everything the channel has ever earned.

One release left — Carmilla, 13 October — and then an EP with no YouTube date on it yet. This deck is about connecting what already exists, not starting something new.

Sources
Every figure is counted from the public YouTube API on 14 Sep 2026 and the Coach plan. The channel has 32 uploads in total, so this is the whole catalogue rather than a sample. Nothing here uses YouTube Studio data.

What we've learned · 1

The biggest number on the channel is the weakest signal on it

0.61%

Observed
Eve has 74,595 views — 26% of everything the channel has ever earned, 2.7× the next best video. It is also the lowest-engaging upload on the channel, running 5.7× below the median official video, with fewer comments than Mother Knows — 36 against 55 — on 7.2× the views.
Means
Whatever drove those views, that audience did not behave like this band's audience. It was reach without a relationship.
So
Judge Carmilla on like rate and comments, not the view counter — and don't build the release plan around repeating what Eve looked like.
Interpretation, not observation
The engagement gap is counted. The cause is not — a promoted or suggested-traffic spike is the most likely explanation for that shape, but the API cannot confirm it. The strategic conclusion is the same either way.

Observed
The Shorts that reach furthest are the personality ones — Hex Girls, witchcraft, gothic literature, band in-jokes — rather than the announcement ones. The best is ZOINKS it's the witches at 3,083; the weakest is a release announcement at 420. Across all 18 they engage at a 2.78% median like rate, the same band as the official videos. And the channel converts viewers to subscribers at 1.05% lifetime — and 0.61% over the last seven days, in a week with nothing uploaded in it at all.
Means
This channel does not have a conversion problem. It has an attendance problem. The voice is already working — there just isn't enough of it, and nothing on the channel currently gathers it into one place.
So
Keep the coven voice and give it somewhere to point: a pinned playlist, an end screen, a next video. That is the difference between a feed and a channel.
