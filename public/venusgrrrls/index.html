Observed
Shorts built on the band's world reach 3× further than release announcements. But announcement Shorts have the highest median like rate of any group at 6.9% — they are seen almost entirely by people who already follow.
Means
The two do completely different jobs, and only one of them recruits. Announcement-led Shorts are not a bad idea executed badly — they are a conversion tool being asked to do a discovery job.
So
Carry Carmilla inside the world rather than announcing it next to the world.
Signal, not law
Three Shorts per comparison group is a small sample and one viral outlier would move it. Treat the direction as real and the multiple as approximate.

On Eve

Eve is the channel's largest video at 74,619 views, 26.3% of all-time viewing and 2.7× the next biggest. It also carries the lowest engagement of any upload — a 0.61% like rate against a 3.46% median for every other official video — and fewer comments than Mother Knows (36 against 55) on 7.2× the views. Observed: the engagement gap. Interpretation: a promoted or suggested-traffic spike is the most likely explanation, but the API cannot confirm it. Either way the deck's recommendation is the same: judge Carmilla on likes and comments rather than the view counter.

Premieres

Verified from scheduledStart and actualStart on the video objects. 3x3 (22 Jan), Eve (24 Apr) and Mother Knows (18 Aug) were all premiered — every 2026 release. Lidocaine (Aug 2023) is the only other premiere in the catalogue.

The Coven

"Welcome to the Coven. Sign up for updates, early access, exclusive art, + more" opens the descriptions of both Eve and Mother Knows, with a laylo.com link. The word also appears in three Shorts, including "hex girls fans come join the coven!!", but none of those Shorts carry the link. End screens, pinned comments and channel sections are not visible from this endpoint, so whether any of those route to it is unknown.

Creative team, from the descriptions

Misha Warren shot six videos including all three 2026 singles. Numen Studios produced 3x3, Ivy Tree and Eve; Romesh Dodangoda produced Eve and Mother Knows. Eve and Mother Knows credit "Direction: Venus Grrrls"; 3x3 carries no direction credit. Hex was filmed in an escape room at Locked in Games, Leeds. Hex and Lidocaine carry flashing-image warnings.

What this deck does not know

The public API carries no format field, so "no live video" is read from titles and durations across 32 uploads — strongly indicated, not proved. It also cannot show traffic sources, watch time, retention or whether any video was promoted. Playlist structure, end screens and channel-section layout are not visible from this endpoint: where the deck calls something missing — the EP playlist above all — it means not visible publicly, and it should be confirmed in Studio before anyone acts on it.
