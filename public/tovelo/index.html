What the campaign set out to do

A kit for every single, and no dark weeks between them.

The ESTRUS YouTube plan, written on 20 May, gave every single the same supporting kit — video, behind-the-scenes, lyric video, visualiser, Shorts cut-downs — and named the weeks in between as work too: a bridge through July and August, then an album ramp at three to five Shorts a week.

What the team should do next

The next content is already booked.

ESTRUS came out three days into a North American tour. The UK and European leg runs 5–19 November — Brixton, the Olympia in Paris, and a home show at Avicii Arena in Stockholm to close. Nine rooms with names, in the weeks a post-album channel usually goes quiet.

What the team should do next

What next.

01

Film the European tour, room by room

Live performance in a named place is the format the plan never listed. Brixton, the Olympia and Stockholm are the obvious three; one song filmed properly per room is a new door into the record every week of November.

02

Give the audience's pick its own video

The album-day visualisers went up together, so their views are a fair vote. The leader is the strongest case for the next proper video.

03

Finish the kit the plan already wrote

The plan's own unfilled items are the cheapest backlog on this page — no new idea needed, only the uploads.

04

No silence longer than a fortnight

Hold every gap under fourteen days through the tour and into the new year.

05

Shorts with Tove in them

The plan said it in May: "personality-driven content on Shorts the same way we are on IG / TikTok." Tour days make that easy — backstage, soundcheck, the city — and none of it needs a release to hang on.

What this page does and does not claim

It does not claim ESTRUS performed better or worse than Dirt Femme — lifetime view data cannot establish that across a four-year age gap. It does not attribute any channel movement to any upload. It has no YouTube Studio data: no impressions, click-through, retention, traffic sources or unique viewers.

It claims how the campaign was run — against its own written plan and against the structure of the last album campaign — and which of the album's songs the audience reached for first, which is the one fair race in the data because the album-day uploads share a birthday.

Method

Shorts
The catalogue endpoint's flag: 62 seconds or under. YouTube now allows Shorts up to three minutes, so a longer vertical upload counts as long-form here — the same rule as the Angus & Julia Stone review, kept for comparability.
Formats
From title text, first match wins: Short → film or live stream (15 min+) → visualiser → lyric → official video → behind the scenes → live & performance → trailer & teaser → audio → other.
Campaign window
1 May 2026 (the Watcher's recorded campaign start) to the read time. The scorecard uses lead-single windows instead so the two eras line up.
Dates
UTC publish dates from the API. A Premiere's publish date can differ from its air time by a day.
The objection to raise first. This measures the artist channel. A lyric video or visualiser on a Topic channel, a Vevo channel or a DSP canvas does not appear, so "missing" means missing from @tovelomusic.
