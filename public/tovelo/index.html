Premiere use is verifiable here: three uploads carry a scheduled start with the broadcast beginning within eight seconds of it. They are the two official videos and the 33-minute album film — the three hero moments, and nothing else in the campaign. Follow-through was tight where it happened: BTS thirteen days after the lead single, the des fleurs video eighteen days after its visualizer, the DNH live cut eight days after its. Then nothing between 7 August and album day.

The next 30–90 days

Follow the audience,
not the calendar.

The campaign has already given every track a surface, and nothing has gone out since. The question now is not how much more to publish — it is which songs have earned a second asset.

A performance capture. A Night In ESTRUS already exists as a format on the channel.

A lyric treatment, as I'm your girl right? got — cheap, and it extends a song's shelf.

World

BTS and world-building around the ESTRUS visual language, which is strong enough to carry it.

Watch

Possibilities, not a schedule. The point of the slide is the selection rule, not the list: build the next chapter around whatever the audience separates out.
