Three bands, three different answers — and two of the three cost nothing. What matters is the system rather than today's ranking: it re-sorts as the album ages, and it tells you what to do either way.

These are decision bands, not statistically objective categories. The top band is unambiguous — an 88% gap to second place. The line under the next three falls on a 32% drop, which is a real break but not the only one in the ranking; drawn slightly differently the middle band would hold more or fewer songs. The bands are a way to decide how much to spend, not a measurement.

source of life · cheapest option first

What a second moment
could cost.

Three ways to answer the signal, at three very different costs. The channel has already shown the shape of the expensive one: des fleurs got a second asset eighteen days after its visualizer, and the song was nowhere near used up.

Low lift · days, not weeks
Cut what exists.
  • Shorts from the existing visualizer
  • Lyric-led vertical moments
  • Re-cut ESTRUS footage around the track
Medium · existing production
Use footage already shot.
  • A standalone live performance, if the song was played at A Night In ESTRUS
  • Lyric video, as I'm your girl right? got
  • An alternate visual cut
Hero · new commission
Make the video.
  • An official video inside the ESTRUS visual language
  • The des fleurs pattern: visualizer first, hero second

Not a recommendation to do all three. The signal decides how far up this ladder the song goes — and the first column is cheap enough to start this week while the decision on the third is still being made.

The Shorts did their job — they kept the channel present on days nothing else went out. But they were made beside the campaign rather than from it. Every long-form asset on this channel contains a moment that could be a Short, and that version costs an edit rather than a shoot.

No claim that Shorts send people to the long-form — that is not visible in public data and we have not tested it. The argument is cost: a Short cut from an asset that already exists is close to free, and it tells you something about the song either way.

Five things, in order
