Campaign architecture · Dirt Femme and ESTRUS

Two ways to build a world.

The world unfolded over time.

15

ESTRUS · 2026

The world arrived at once.

11

The full album was given a visual surface immediately, so every track was available to be chosen from the first day.

The next 30–90 days

Follow the audience,
not the calendar.

The campaign has already given every track a surface. The question now is not how much more to publish — it is which songs have earned a second asset.
