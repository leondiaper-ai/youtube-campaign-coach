Every view figure on both pages is a live counter read at that moment and drifts by a few hundred within the hour; they are accurate to the stated read time, not frozen. 84.3% coverage is unusually good for this kind of read, so share-of-catalogue statements stand on firmer ground than they often do. The remaining 522,044,141 views sit in content that is no longer public, and nothing here claims to account for it.

The two limitations

1 · ESTRUS and Dirt Femme view totals are not comparable

A matched campaign clock puts assets published in the same relative window side by side. But Dirt Femme's assets were published in 2022 and have been accumulating for about four years; ESTRUS's have had between seventeen days and five months.

A matched window of publication is not a matched window of accumulation. And it cannot be corrected: the API returns a lifetime counter and nothing else, so there is no way to ask what Dirt Femme had done at day 17 — nobody recorded it in 2022.

So the deck compares structure (cadence, format mix, how each visual world was built) and shares, which age does not distort. No cross-era view total appears anywhere on it.

2 · There is no ESTRUS release-week velocity data

The Watcher's daily collection for this channel failed during the week that mattered:

  • The channel snapshot has been frozen since 22 September at 3,330,599,183 views and 3,880,000 subscribers — thirteen days with no movement recorded.
  • The per-video daily series stops on 17 September, the day before the album.
  • Earlier readings are catch-ups, not daily: 8 September records +33,582,602 in a single reading covering fourteen days.

Every ESTRUS figure in the deck is therefore a lifetime total as at 5 October, with no visible shape behind it. The deck makes no claim about momentum, velocity, first-week performance or growth rate, because any such claim would have been invented.

This is the only cross-era figure the deck uses, because a share is not distorted by age the way a total is. Two campaigns with opposite Shorts strategies — 86 against 38 — land within a point of each other on where the viewing comes from.

Arithmetic: ESTRUS 10,478,289 long-form of 11,310,407 total = 92.63%. Dirt Femme 22,915,899 of 24,984,527 = 91.72%. The totals themselves are stated here for traceability and are deliberately absent from the deck for the reason in limitation 1.

A second window caveat: −127 is ESTRUS's lead single, but Dirt Femme's lead (No One Dies From Love) sits at D−163, outside the window. So the matched window cuts Dirt Femme's own lead asset out. Measured instead from D−163, Dirt Femme has 114 uploads and 51,689,818 long-form views. Whichever boundary is chosen, one campaign is disadvantaged; there is no correct answer, only a stated one.

Ten of the eleven are long-form; one is not. a lot of feelings, no solutions was published as a Short. It is counted in the set above because it is part of the same release-day drop, and the shares in the table are of all eleven. Against the ten long-form visualizers alone, source of life is 60.5% of 1,671,519 — the conclusion does not turn on which basis is used, but the basis is stated rather than left to be guessed.

The deck frames this as audience selection, not as ten failures. Seventeen days is not long, every track was given the same surface on the same day, and one has moved. That is a signal about demand, not a verdict on ten pieces of work.

des fleurs — sequence, not cause

Visualizer 12 June, 899,673. Official video 30 June, 4,978,233. Eighteen days apart.

The deck says only that an early visual treatment did not exhaust demand for a larger hero asset. It does not say the visualizer caused the video's performance — nothing in public data can show whether it helped, hurt or did nothing.

Dirt Femme's Scenes

Fifteen numbered long-form Scenes released out of sequence between June 2022 and August 2023 — fourteen months. Together 12,043,420 views, mean 802,895. There were also twelve Scene N Shorts posted 5–9 October 2022 as an album countdown; those did 5,279–20,320 views each.

What this read does not claim

  • That ESTRUS is out- or under-performing Dirt Femme. See limitation 1.
  • Anything about release-week momentum. See limitation 2.
  • That the quiet year cost anything. One upload in 2025 is a fact; its consequences are not visible from outside.
  • That the des fleurs visualizer drove the official video. Sequence only.
  • That the Scenes approach beat the visualizer approach, or the reverse. They are measured at four years and seventeen days.
  • Anything about streaming, radio or touring. No DSP data was used; YouTube views are not a proxy for consumption elsewhere.
