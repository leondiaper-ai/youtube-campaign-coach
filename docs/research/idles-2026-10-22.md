## Label considerations

Keep major videos; consider a lyric video where it serves the song, a selective owned performance and useful making-of footage. Review existing material before commissioning. Prepare the homepage and a few worthwhile follow-ups around priority tracks. Keep partner performances accessible in a live playlist. These are proposals grounded in examples, not confirmed plans. Use Studio watch time, returning viewers and traffic sources to test the response.
