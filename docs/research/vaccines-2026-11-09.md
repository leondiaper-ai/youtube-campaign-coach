## Proposed strategy

Keep a coherent visualiser world. Reserve one distinct follow-up while commissioning the primary; use a provisional +7–14 day slot as an operating test. Price a small-room capture of one new song and one catalogue favourite before committing to a series. Pilot two Shorts and one Community conversation per week over six weeks; additional long-form depends on response and cost. Review new assets at Day 7 and Day 28; use Studio before scaling.

Working creative directions: Postcards from the next record; The small room, the big chorus; What did you expect next? New campaign artwork and artist approvals supersede the retrospective art direction.

Partner priorities reflect judgement, not eligibility: Weekly Release Support first, then explore Top Fans, London OOH and Station; Music Night / Release Party is a stretch. The screenshot's six-week commitment is an assumption to reconfirm. Programme the catalogue as a playlist while discussing Station eligibility, delivery and rights.

All four references were read: CHVRCHES for clear narrative/continuity, Ezra for asset-count and rhythm analysis, IDLES for live destinations, Amyl for low-band-time programming. Their benchmarks are not transplanted into the Vaccines review. The new visual identity uses the Vaccines' own thumbnails and a pink/sky/ochre/wine palette.
