**Purpose.** Identify artist-specific, evidence-led YouTube strategy for the Oct 2026 – Jan 2027 album campaign. Not generic best practice.

## 1. Executive findings

Three. Each one should change a decision.

### Finding 1 — Palaye's between-hero layer is Shorts, and at Palaye's scale Shorts do not pay

**OBSERVED.** Since 1 Jan 2024 the Palaye Royale artist channel has posted **135 Shorts**. Their combined lifetime views are **1,384,951**, median **4,068**.

In the same period, one single music video on Sumerian — *Showbiz* — has **2,364,949** views.

> **One music video has out-viewed 135 Shorts and all 12 episodes of Royal Television Season 05 combined (1,654,656).**

Shorts are **90%** of everything the artist channel has uploaded in 24 months and account for a rounding error of the viewing.

**This is scale-dependent, and that is the important part.** YUNGBLUD posted **291 Shorts** in 2025 with a median of **150,728** and a total of **98,163,017**. At 3.87m subscribers Shorts are a genuine viewing tier. At Palaye's 272,000 they are not — the same behaviour returns ~37× less per upload.

**INTERPRETATION.** Palaye have adopted the upload *pattern* of a much larger artist without the subscriber base that makes it pay. The effort is real; the return is not. I cannot show what Shorts do for discovery or subscriber growth — public data cannot see that — so this is a claim about *observed viewing only*.

**RECOMMENDATION.** Stop treating Shorts as the connective tissue of the campaign. They are a promotional surface for assets that exist, not a destination in themselves.

### Finding 2 — Live performance is the missing music destination between the hero videos

**OBSERVED, three independent sources:**

**YUNGBLUD.** His longest 2025 gap between hero music videos — **176 days**, Zombie (30 May) to Wild Woman (22 Nov) — was carried by live music. The two biggest assets in that window were live:

| Asset | Views |
|---|---:|
| Changes (Live From Villa Park / Back To The Beginning) | **18,155,255** |
| Zombie (Live from BLUDFEST) | **9,058,552** |
| *His own 2025 music-video median, for reference* | *9,412,699* |

One live performance **beat his music-video median by 1.9×**. Another matched it. He also runs a *named, recurring* live series — **Live From Hansa Studios**, 11 assets, full performances at 3,543,384 / 1,825,060 / 431,785.

**Bad Omens.** A 505,000-subscriber band with only **24 public uploads**. Their non-Shorts output is almost entirely live: **5,822,087** (V.A.N live in Europe), **2,907,050**, **2,029,128**. Live is not a supporting format for them — it *is* the channel.

**Palaye themselves.** The best-performing non-music-video assets ever posted to the artist channel are four **Live Acoustic** videos from 2020:

| Asset | Views |
|---|---:|
| Mr. Doctor Man (Live Acoustic) | 283,747 |
| Hang On To Yourself (Live Acoustic) | 157,980 |
| Dying In A Hot Tub (Live From a Hot Tub) | 109,675 |
| Black Sheep (Live Acoustic) | 101,121 |
| **Median** | **133,828** |

That median is **6× Royal Television's current-season median of 22,318** — despite the acoustic videos being five years older than Season 05, which cuts *against* them on age.

**Palaye have posted 14 live-or-performance videos in the channel's entire history, and none since 2021.**

**INTERPRETATION.** Between the hero videos there is currently nowhere for a *music* audience to go. Royal Television is a story destination; Shorts are fragments. The format that repeatedly carries other artists between releases — and that Palaye's own history says works for them — is a performance of the song.

**RECOMMENDATION.** Add one live/performance music asset between hero videos. This is the single biggest gap in the plan.

### Finding 3 — Royal Television is 69 episodes, not 84, and it should be the story layer, not the calendar

**OBSERVED.** 84 uploads match "Royal Television". **15 of them are 17–45-second promo clips** (median duration 40s, median 6,192 views) advertising the real episodes. The actual series is **69 episodes**.

Real-episode medians by season:

| Season | Episodes | Period | Median | Median duration |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 01 | 20 | 2018 | 77,142 | 16m |
| 02 | 3 | 2018 | 89,532 | 22m |
| 03 | 17 | 2018–19 | 37,983 | 12m |
| 04 | 17 | 2021–23 | 25,181 | 16m |
| **05** | **12** | **2025** | **22,318** | **27m** |

Against Palaye's contemporary music-video baseline on Sumerian (2024 campaign median **1,182,195**; 2025 singles median **624,767**), a Season 05 episode runs at roughly **2–4%** of a music video.

**Is that weak?** No — and this is where I expected to find against the deck and didn't. Bring Me The Horizon's episodic content runs at **1.0%** of their own contemporary MV median. Palaye's runs higher. **Royal Television is ordinary-to-slightly-better than peer episodic content.** The problem is that episodic is a low-yield format *for everyone*, and YUNGBLUD has effectively abandoned it — **8 episodic uploads all-time, zero since 2024.**

Season 05 also lost its own cadence: weekly from 28 Jan, then gaps of 14, 21 and 70 days, ending 15 July.

**INTERPRETATION.** Royal Television is a genuine owned asset and worth restarting — but not as an always-on weekly commitment, which is what it was in Season 05 and what it failed to sustain. Its value is narrative, not volume.

**RECOMMENDATION.** Four episodes, one per hero moment. Not a weekly series.

**INTERPRETATION, moderate confidence.** Yes, but not through episodic storytelling. He builds it through **repeated named contexts** — Hansa Studios, BLUDFEST — that recur across releases and give live assets a franchise identity. The world is a set of *places*, not a series.

### Methodology for comparison

Raw lifetime views across artists of different scale and upload age are meaningless. So each non-MV format is expressed as a **ratio to that same artist's own music-video median in the same time window** (2024-01-01 onward). This controls for artist scale and roughly for era. It does not fully control for age *within* the window, and where a ratio rests on n<3 I have marked it rather than reported it.

### Ratios to own contemporary MV median

| Format | YUNGBLUD | BMTH | Motionless In White |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Live / performance | **0.147** | **0.190** | n<3 |
| Lyric video | 0.002 | 0.052 | 0.031 |
| Episodic | — (none since 2024) | 0.010 | n<3 |
| BTS | 0.009 | n<3 | n<3 |
| Shorts | 0.018 | 0.003 | 0.006 |
| Alternate versions | n<3 | n<3 | 0.016 |

**OBSERVED.** Live is the only non-MV format that clears 10% of the artist's own music-video median in more than one catalogue. Lyric videos, BTS, episodic and Shorts all sit in the low single digits of a percent.

### Two peers that don't work as benchmarks — and why

**Falling In Reverse** is a poor methodological comparison. Its channel is functionally Ronnie Radke's personal streaming and merchandise channel — 70 of its uploads are livestreams with titles like *"Live with Restream"*, *"9 SIGNED GUITARS LEFT"* (6,061 views) and *"I LOST MY LAWSUIT WITH ANTHONY FANTANO"*. It has **no music videos since 2024**. It tells us nothing transferable about campaign programming. **Excluded from the format ratios.**

**Sleep Token** is the interesting counter-example rather than a benchmark. **5% Shorts share, active in only 4 of the last 24 months, a 356-day silence** — and 1.29m subscribers with 1.01bn lifetime views. Their non-MV content is instrumentals and visualisers. **INTERPRETATION:** total scarcity is a viable strategy, but it is an identity built over years around anonymity and it is not available to Palaye mid-campaign. I raise it only to be honest that "programme consistently" is not a universal law — Sleep Token contradicts it and wins.

**Substitution recommended:** I would swap Falling In Reverse out and keep **Bad Omens** despite its tiny upload count, because a 24-upload / 505k-subscriber channel whose entire non-Shorts output is live performance is directly relevant to Finding 2.

## 4. Palaye's existing strengths

**OBSERVED:**

1. **Royal Television — 69 real episodes across 5 seasons, 3,308,906 views.** Genuinely rare. Most peers have nothing comparable; BMTH's episodic run is 16 uploads.
2. **A proven live/acoustic format** — the 2020 sessions at a 133,828 median, still the channel's best non-MV work.
3. **A working label partnership.** The Sumerian *Death or Glory* campaign (2024) delivered a music-video median of **1,182,195** across five videos, topping at *Showbiz* 2,364,949.
4. **Recent evidence the audience is still live.** The 2025 singles took 718,736 and 530,798 on Sumerian.

**INTERPRETATION.** Palaye's problem is not audience and not assets. It is that the artist channel currently holds only the *lowest-yield* half of what they make.

## 5. Format hierarchy

### TIER 1 — HERO · Official music video (Sumerian)
- **Role:** the release itself; the reach event.
- **When:** the four supplied dates.
- **Why:** 2024 campaign median 1,182,195 vs anything on the artist channel at ~4k–22k.
- **Evidence:** Sumerian Palaye MVs 2024–25.
- **Confidence: HIGH.**

### TIER 2 — MUSIC DESTINATION · Live / performance (artist channel)
- **Role:** the second music moment in each gap. Gives the artist channel a reason to exist for a *music* audience.
- **When:** roughly 2–3 weeks after each hero video.
- **Why:** the only non-MV format clearing 10% of own-MV median across multiple peers (YUNGBLUD 0.147, BMTH 0.190); Bad Omens' whole channel; Palaye's own 2020 sessions at 6× current RTV.
- **Evidence:** §2, §3, Finding 2.
- **Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH.** The peer pattern is consistent; Palaye's own supporting data is five years old.

### TIER 3 — STORY · Royal Television (artist channel)
- **Role:** narrative connective tissue. Four episodes, one per moment — **not** weekly.
- **When:** in the week before or after each hero video.
- **Why:** it exists, it is owned, and it beats peer episodic content relative to own-MV baseline. But it is a 2–4% format and Season 05 could not hold a weekly cadence.
- **Evidence:** §1 Finding 3.
- **Confidence: MEDIUM.**

### TIER 4 — SUPPORT · Shorts
- **Role:** cut *from* Tier 1–3 assets. Never commissioned separately.
- **Why:** 135 Shorts → 1,384,951 total. As an origination format at 272k subs it does not repay the effort.
- **Confidence: HIGH** on the viewing observation; **LOW** on any discovery effect, which public data cannot see.

### TIER 5 — RESPONSE-LED ONLY · Lyric, visualiser, alternate versions
- **Role:** made *only* when a specific song is already responding.
- **Why:** YUNGBLUD's recent lyric median is **22,200** — 0.2% of his MV median. His strong visualiser number is one Aerosmith collaboration, not a format effect.
- **Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH** that these should not be made by default.

## 6. Campaign architecture

The principle: **always-on should not mean always working.** Four hero moments, four gaps, roughly **three meaningful artist-channel assets per gap** — not thirty.

```
2 OCT · LOST IN TRANSLATION (MV, Sumerian) + album announce
   └─ RTV episode 1 — the record, why now          (artist channel, w/c 6 Oct)
   └─ LIVE performance of Lost In Translation      (artist channel, ~20 Oct)
27 OCT · SINGLE TWO (MV, Sumerian)
   └─ LIVE performance                             (~10 Nov)
   └─ RTV episode 2 — on the road                  (mid Nov)
19 NOV · SINGLE THREE (MV, Sumerian)
   └─ RTV episode 3 — making the album             (early Dec)
   └─ LIVE / session piece                         (early Jan — the 64-day gap)
22 JAN · ALBUM + FOCUS TRACK (MV, Sumerian)
   └─ RTV episode 4 — the album                    (late Jan)
   └─ LIVE — full session or show                  (Feb)
25 JAN · DELUXE
```

**The 19 Nov → 22 Jan gap is 64 days** and is the one that matters. It is the only gap long enough to lose the audience, and it is where YUNGBLUD's evidence is strongest — his 176-day gap was held by live music.

Shorts are cut from the live shoots and the RTV episodes. **Nothing on this plan is commissioned solely to be a Short.**

## 7. What not to do

1. **Do not commission lyric videos or visualisers by default.** YUNGBLUD's recent lyric median is 22,200 — 0.2% of his MV median. Make one only if a specific song is already moving.
2. **Do not restart Royal Television as a weekly series.** Season 05 tried and broke — weekly from 28 Jan, then gaps of 14, 21 and 70 days.
3. **Do not scale up Shorts origination.** 135 Shorts returned 1,384,951 total. The pattern works for YUNGBLUD at 3.87m subs; it does not transfer at 272k.
4. **Do not move the music videos off Sumerian.** Not tested here, but nothing in this research argues against the existing split, and Sumerian's 2024 median of 1,182,195 is the reach the campaign depends on.
5. **Do not copy YUNGBLUD's upload volume.** 353 uploads in 24 months is a different resourcing model.

## 8. Deck implications

Maximum three. I would not rebuild the deck.

1. **Slide 7 ("Every video needs a next") currently lists seven formats as a flat menu with "Have it / Opportunity" tags.** The research says they are not peers — live belongs in a tier above Shorts, and lyric/visualiser belong below both. Convert the menu into the three-tier hierarchy from §5.
2. **Add live/performance as a named recommendation.** It is currently one tile among seven. It is the strongest evidence-backed gap in the plan and the deck does not argue for it.
3. **Correct "84 episodes" to 69.** 15 of the 84 are 40-second promo clips. The deck's own figure overstates the asset. Season 05's real-episode median is **22,318**, not the 12,682 currently implied — which is *better* for the argument, and correct.

## 9. Methodology and limitations

**Method.** One API pull, 4 Sep 2026. All seven channels verified by `channel.title`. Uploads playlists taken uncapped. 2,195 uploads classified by title pattern, duration and the `isShort` flag, in priority order, with alternate-version and film categories added after inspecting what the unclassified bucket actually contained.

**`wasLive` is not a live-content signal.** YouTube reports Premieres as live broadcasts. YUNGBLUD's *Zombie (Official Music Video)* — 33.9m views — carries `wasLive: true`. Every Royal Television episode does too. Classifying on that flag would have labelled hero music videos as livestreams. Title rules run first; `wasLive` is used only for unclassified items over 15 minutes.

**Limitations, in order of how much they should worry you:**

- **No per-video history exists anywhere in this system.** Verified in source. D7/D30/D90 cannot be reconstructed for any video on any channel. Every figure here is a *current lifetime snapshot*. No age adjustment is possible beyond restricting comparisons to matched windows.
- **Views were never divided by age**, and no "views per day" metric appears anywhere. Where uploads of different ages are compared I have said so.
- **Small n.** YUNGBLUD's 2024+ music-video baseline is n=5; BMTH's is n=3. Ratios built on these are directional, not precise. Anything resting on n<3 is marked rather than reported.
- **Classification is imperfect.** Roughly 8% of uploads land in `MIDFORM?`/`LONGFORM?`/`OTHER`. Palaye's tour-numbered series ("No. 29", "No. 30") is counted as episodic, which is arguable.
- **No Studio access.** Nothing here can speak to retention, traffic sources, browse/suggested, unique viewers, subscriber conversion, or whether Shorts feed long-form. **No claim in this document asserts any of those.**
- **Correlation only.** That live assets carry big view counts in YUNGBLUD's gaps does not establish that live *caused* anything, or that the same assets would perform for Palaye at a seventh of the subscriber base.
- **Sumerian coverage.** The Sumerian pull returned 1,000 uploads, of which 58 are Palaye. That may not be the channel's complete catalogue, so the Palaye-on-Sumerian set could be incomplete.
